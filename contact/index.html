Questions

Frequently asked questions.

01What does Sahem actually do?
We design, build, and deploy the operational intelligence systems that government agencies use to run their physical estate — national parks, royal reserves, heritage sites, environmental operations. Our first delivered engagement was WildMotion at Zimbabwe Parks and Wildlife Management Authority.
02Have you done this before?
Yes. WildMotion at Zimbabwe Parks and Wildlife Management Authority — a full operational intelligence system across their national ranger network. It is our first delivered engagement and is in active use today.
03How are you different from McKinsey or BCG?
We do not write strategy reports. The top-tier firms will tell you what should be built. We design and build the system itself, then install it inside your agency. We often do the work the strategy firms recommended.
04How long does delivery take?
Operational Discovery is ten working days. A pilot deployment is eight to twelve weeks. A full multi-site rollout is six to twelve months depending on scope.
05What does this cost?
Discovery is SAR 65K–95K. A pilot is SAR 250K–500K. A full platform build is SAR 1M–3M depending on coverage. Annual operational intelligence support is SAR 200K–500K. Roughly a quarter to a third of comparable Big-4 scope.
06Are you Saudi-licensed?
Our MISA application is in progress. Full licensing will be in place ahead of any Saudi contracting. We will not sign engagements with Saudi agencies until we are properly licensed to operate.
