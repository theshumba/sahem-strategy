Privacy Policy.

Last updated 18 May 2026.

Who we are.

Sahem Strategy (“Sahem”, “we”, “us”) is a consulting practice operating from the United Kingdom and providing operational intelligence services to government agencies. For the purposes of UK GDPR, Sahem Strategy is the data controller for any personal data collected through this website.

What we collect.

We collect personal data only when you choose to provide it — typically through the contact form on this site. That data is limited to your name, organisation, email address, country, and the contents of any message you write to us.

We do not currently use cookies or analytics on this site. We do not track individual visitors. We do not buy or sell personal data.

How we use it.

We use the information you provide solely to respond to your enquiry, to evaluate a potential engagement, and to communicate with you about Sahem services you have expressed interest in. We do not send marketing emails unless you explicitly request to be added to a Sahem mailing list.

Where we store it.

Form submissions are routed via Formspree (formspree.io) and stored alongside our standard email infrastructure. Both providers are GDPR-compliant. We retain enquiry data only as long as the engagement conversation is active, plus a reasonable archival period thereafter.

Your rights.

Under UK GDPR you have the right to request access to your personal data, request correction or deletion, and object to our processing of it. To exercise any of these rights, email hello@sahemstrategy.com. We respond within thirty days.

Changes.

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated by updating the "Last updated" date above. If you have given us personal data and the changes are material to that data, we will email you.

Contact.

For privacy enquiries or to exercise your data rights, contact us at hello@sahemstrategy.com.
