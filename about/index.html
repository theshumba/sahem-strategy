About

A small practice designed for sovereign work.

Sahem Strategy is an operational-intelligence practice. We design and deploy the systems sovereign agencies need to run their physical estate — and we deliver them inside the real constraints of public-sector operations.

Principles

How we work.

We build, we don't advise.

A Sahem engagement ends with a deployed system inside your institution. Not a slide deck, not a report.

Embedded, not remote.

Every engagement begins on-site. Operational realities cannot be discovered from a conference room overseas.

Earn the next phase.

We scope deliberately. Discovery earns a pilot. A pilot earns a platform. A platform earns ongoing stewardship. No oversold engagements.

Institutional, not individual.

Our systems must survive staff rotation and leadership change. We design for the institution, not for the person who signed the contract.
