Conservation / Royal Reserves / Heritage / Environmental Ops / Sovereign Infrastructure

Sahem is a sovereign operations studio. We design, build, and deploy the operational intelligence systems that government agencies use to run their physical estate — conservation reserves, heritage sites, environmental operations, sovereign infrastructure. Our first delivered engagement was WildMotion at Zimbabwe Parks and Wildlife Management Authority. We are taking the same approach into the Gulf, starting in Riyadh from late 2026.
