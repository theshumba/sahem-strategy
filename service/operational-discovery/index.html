Benefit

A short, sharp engagement before any big commitment.

Most sovereign agencies do not need another strategy report. They need someone to spend ten working days inside their actual operation, watch how rangers and inspectors really work, and identify where operational visibility breaks down.

That is Operational Discovery. We embed inside your team for two working weeks. We watch. We talk to field staff, regional supervisors, and leadership. We map the operational nervous system. Then we write a brief that names the three to five highest-leverage things to fix — with a costed Phase 2 proposal attached.

  • Embedded on-site discovery, not remote interviews
  • Operational reality mapped against your reporting structure
  • Three to five concrete recommendations, ranked by leverage
  • A costed pilot proposal, ready to greenlight
  • SAR 65,000–95,000, ten working days

Discovery is the most under-rated phase of a sovereign engagement. Ten days inside the operation produces more useful insight than ten weeks of remote analysis. It is also how we earn trust — by spending time with the people doing the actual work.
